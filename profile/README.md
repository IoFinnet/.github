# Welcome

**io.finnet** is a digital asset management platform with proven security and intuitive features. 

As an organization, you may be interested in our 
institutional MPC self-custody product with customizable policy-based risk controls, 
our digital fiat clearing solutions, 
or you may be an individual exploring the frontier of financial technology.

## What is io.finnet?

io.finnet provides a suite of solutions unrivaled in the market.

- **Bulletproof Self-Custody** – Leveraging trustless Multi-Party Computation ([tMPC](https://docs.iofinnet.com/docs/trustless-mpc-tmpc)) to eliminate single points of failure and redefine digital asset security with full web3 and cross-exchange access.

- **24/7 Instant Fiat Settlement** – Our private blockchain io.network powers instant fiat settlements anytime, anywhere, always secured with multi-approver MPC and institutional-grade smart contracts.

- **Comprehensive Asset Support** – Effortlessly manage BTC, ETH, ERC-20 tokens and 100,000's many more [supported assets](https://docs.iofinnet.com/docs/supported-blockchains-and-assets).

- **Battle-Tested Security** – Trusted by over 100 businesses, safeguarding millions in self-custodied assets and powering huge transaction volume. Our founders created the very first independent audited implementations of ECDSA and EDDSA threshold signature (TSS) schemes, and have backgrounds as software architects at large exchanges.

Built with scalability and trustlessness in mind, io.finnet redefines digital asset management for businesses and individuals alike.

## Why io.finnet?
